About AMH Tourism

Travel Partner Built for Complex Environments

AMH Tourism is a UAE-owned, IATA-accredited travel management company with over a decade of experience managing corporate travel, event logistics and executive hospitality across the GCC and beyond.

We serve Multinational corporations, Government entities, Technology companies, Real estate, Mobility, international trade and Cargo organisations Media and broadcast operators and many more that operate across multiple markets simultaneously. Our proposition rests on three things: genuine global expertise, structured technology and people who act immediately when plans change.
